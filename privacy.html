Workout Order Advisor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Workout Order Advisor is an independent experimental fitness application designed to help users explore how the order of two workouts in one day may relate to their training and recovery. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by WHOOP.

Information we access

With your authorization, the application may access WHOOP workout records (including workout types, times, heart-rate summaries and strain), sleep information, recovery measurements, and physiological cycle data. The application uses only the scopes you explicitly authorize.

How information is used

We use this information to compare workout sequences and generate exploratory, personalized workout-order insights. These insights are not medical advice, and associations in past data do not establish causation.

Storage and security

During the initial private development phase, authorized WHOOP data and authentication tokens are intended to be processed and stored in a developer-controlled environment. Tokens must not be shared publicly. If hosted storage or third-party processing is introduced, this policy will be updated before that use begins.

Sharing

We do not sell WHOOP data. We do not share identifiable WHOOP data with advertisers. Data may be handled by infrastructure providers if the app is deployed using their services; details will be added to this policy before such deployment.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only while needed for the experimental application. You may revoke the app's WHOOP authorization from your WHOOP account. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously stored data. To request deletion of stored information, contact the application operator using the contact method supplied when the application is made available.

Your choices

You may decline authorization or revoke it later. Without access to WHOOP data, personalized analysis will not work.

Changes

This policy may change as the project develops. The updated date will be revised whenever substantive changes are made.

Contact

Before sharing this app with anyone else, the developer must add a working contact email address here for privacy requests.
